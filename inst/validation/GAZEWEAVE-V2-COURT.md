# Frozen GazeWeave v2 synthetic court

Status: frozen before final-seed execution
Protocol version: 1
Date frozen: 2026-08-15
Final seed: `20260816`
Pilot/debug seed: `17`
Mote gate: `bd-01M03RQQQ2JQCWZ887C3D60FH3`

## Purpose

This court decides whether Transport v2 and Replay are sufficiently correct,
stable, and useful to advance to real-data controls. It does not establish that
either method is universally superior. The method families are deliberately
heterogeneous and include cases favorable to each engine and cases generated
by neither engine.

The protocol, perturbation levels, summaries, and advancement rules below are
fixed before running seed `20260816`. Debugging with seed `17` may repair code
or a violated implementation contract, but may not change a threshold because
of comparative performance. Any later analysis is labelled an addendum.

## Units, candidates, and cross-fitting

- Coordinates are expressed in degrees of visual angle on a `24 x 18` degree
  screen.
- Each candidate court contains six image templates and two independently
  generated replications per family. The implementation may run a smaller
  smoke configuration, but only the declared final configuration can produce
  the gate verdict.
- Families are `transport_favouring`, `replay_favouring`, and
  `neutral_misspecified`.
- The source table contains a matched signal row and an independently
  generated background-null row for each nominal item.
- Models and preprocessing are fitted only from signal rows in outer training
  folds. Both signal and null rows are evaluated in the held-out folds.
- Candidate pools, uniform priors, outer folds, participant-level registration
  policy, and output metrics are identical across engines and comparators.
- Frozen scores and supervised/calibrated scores remain separate. A frozen
  compatibility weight is never called calibrated information.

## Frozen engines and comparators

- Transport v2 uses directed next-two ordinal relations, coverage
  `{0.5, 0.75, 1}`, omission penalties `{0.25, 0.75, 1.5}`, Gaussian-mixture
  widths `{0.75, 1.5, 3}` degrees, entropy continuation `{0.05, 0.015}`, and
  one deterministic start in the predictive court. The dedicated stability
  audit repeats declared cases with both deterministic starts.
- Replay uses a 48-bin duration grid, maximum local skip two, Student
  emissions with four degrees of freedom, and the transition grid encoded in
  the validation script.
- Replay likelihood temperature is fitted on two inner item folds within each
  outer-training set, with a fixed unit-scale Gaussian prior on log temperature.
  Information and log loss are computed from normalized log probabilities so
  posterior underflow cannot create infinite evidence.
- The default held-out warp is participant-level isotropic contraction about
  screen centre plus participant translation. Every candidate receives the
  same outer-training warp.
- Comparators are frozen raw and registered MultiMatch dimensions, frozen raw
  and registered fixed multiscale density, registered order-free elastic
  consensus, and nested-ridge registered MultiMatch and density composites.
  Missing optional dependencies are explicit skips.

## Data-generating families

- `transport_favouring`: duration-weighted partial spatial support with unequal
  fixation counts, mild local order damage, and no privileged frame-by-frame
  replay clock.
- `replay_favouring`: locally forward replay fragments, occasional restarts,
  omissions, and candidate-independent background fixations.
- `neutral_misspecified`: noisy duration-weighted occupation of item-specific
  regions with local runs, generated by neither fitted likelihood.

All signal families include held-out contraction and calibration translation.
Null rows use the same fixation-count and duration distributions but are drawn
from participant-level central background gaze independently of the nominal
candidate.

## Representation and perturbation court

Representation checks cover:

1. splitting and merging adjacent identical fixations;
2. a reasonable detector variant that coalesces sub-threshold adjacent points;
3. duration-preserving interval resampling;
4. equivalent pixel and visual-degree coordinates.

The perturbation design is a balanced fractional factorial plus one-factor
anchors. It independently changes contraction, translation, spatial noise,
uniform time dilation, local swaps, block reordering, complete reversal,
deletion, insertion, central bias, fixation count, duration concentration, and
candidate difficulty. Main effects are reported as changes from the
unperturbed matched score. Key interactions are contraction by noise, deletion
by insertion, order damage by block reordering, and central bias by candidate
difficulty.

Expected qualitative controls are preregistered:

- uniform time dilation must not change either engine score beyond `1e-8`;
- exact adjacent splitting/merging must not change Replay or coalesced
  Transport beyond `1e-8`;
- equivalent unit conversion must change scores by at most `1e-6`;
- complete reversal must reduce Replay support relative to preserved order;
- fixed-density order damage must not materially improve density;
- contraction correction must reduce scale error without improving null
  candidate identification.

## Metrics

For each supervised method the court reports held-out mean
`gaze_info_bits`, log loss in nats, top-one fractional credit, mean template
rank, Brier score, and equal-width expected calibration error with five bins.
Frozen methods report compatibility metrics and rank without interpreting them
as information.

Signal detection is evaluated at a matched false-positive rate. For each
method, the threshold is the empirical 95th percentile of held-out null
scores, using the conservative upper order statistic. Reported null error is
the fraction above that threshold with ties counted as half; power is the
analogous signal fraction. This small synthetic court estimates operating
behavior, not a population p-value.

Parameter recovery includes contraction scale, Replay background occupancy,
and Transport coverage. Runtime, R garbage-collector high-water memory,
post-fit used memory, and serialized fit size are reported by engine. The
pilot rejected allocation tracing because its per-allocation instrumentation
materially changed Transport runtime.

## Stability court

- Transport is run with both deterministic starts. Required tolerances are
  maximum scientific-score start spread `<= 0.02`, barycentric start RMSE
  `<= 0.25` degrees, candidate-order score difference `<= 1e-8`, and finite
  stationarity diagnostics.
- Replay candidate-order score and log-likelihood differences must be
  `<= 1e-10`; posterior rows must sum to one within `1e-10`; 32-, 48-, and
  64-bin sensitivity must preserve the true-candidate rank and change the
  per-bin contrastive predictive log likelihood (true candidate minus the
  log-mean nonmatch likelihood) by no more than `0.15` nats.
- At least 99% of final engine evaluations must be finite and declared
  converged.

## Advancement and default rules

Advance to real-data controls only if:

1. all representation, fold-isolation, candidate-order, normalization, and
   stability contracts pass;
2. null error is at most `0.075` for each candidate-probability engine;
3. both engines have positive mean signal information in at least two of the
   three families and top-one credit above the `1/6` prior overall;
4. at least one GazeWeave engine improves held-out log loss over the supervised
   density composite in the fixed-density/order-sensitive control;
5. contraction recovery has absolute scale error at most `0.08`, and
   registration does not increase null top-one credit by more than `0.05`;
6. the complete result bundle, configuration, seed, and session information
   can be regenerated within the declared numerical tolerances.

Replay becomes the provisional encoding-to-recall default only if it passes
the gate and its pooled log loss over `replay_favouring` plus
`neutral_misspecified` is no more than `0.05` nats worse than Transport, while
also outperforming Transport on the local-replay/chunk control. Otherwise no
directional default is selected. Transport remains the symmetric API for
comparisons without a privileged encoding-to-recall direction regardless of
this decision.

No universal-superiority claim is permitted. A failed gate yields a bounded
failure report and an explicitly versioned addendum; it does not authorize
post-hoc retuning of the frozen court.

## Debug addendum A: likelihood resolution diagnostic

Pilot seed `17` revealed that absolute per-bin Replay likelihood includes a
large candidate-independent discretization offset. The frozen `0.15`-nat
threshold is unchanged, but the diagnostic is explicitly contrastive: the
true-candidate likelihood minus the log-mean nonmatch likelihood, divided by
grid size. This is the quantity that enters item identification after common
offsets cancel. No score threshold or data-generating setting was changed.

## Debug addendum B: Transport numerical budget

Pilot seed `17` produced finite scores but only `95.8%` all-profile convergence
with a `2e-4` relative-objective tolerance after 60 iterations. Before the
final seed was run, the declared solver tolerance was set to `5e-4`, still 40
times smaller than the frozen `0.02` scientific-score start-spread tolerance,
and the final maximum was set to 80 iterations. Candidate-level work was
parallelized across at most four fork workers without changing starts,
objectives, or candidate order. No scientific gate threshold or generator was
changed.

## Debug addendum C: Replay calibration separation and log-domain evidence

The first regeneration at final seed `20260816` after adding Replay likelihood
temperature calibration failed the unchanged null-error gate (`0.0972`). The
temperature had been fitted on the same outer-training paths used to estimate
Replay emissions and transitions. Perfect separation drove it toward its lower
bound, causing numerical zero/one candidate probabilities and tied ceiling null
scores.

Before rerunning the final seed, calibration was changed at the algorithm
level: candidate scores now come from two inner item folds, log temperature has
a fixed unit-scale Gaussian prior centred at zero, and information/log loss are
computed from normalized log probabilities. Training strata with fewer than
four candidate items record a temperature-one fallback. The final seed,
generator, candidates, perturbations, and all gate thresholds were unchanged.
The regenerated Replay null error was `0.0417`; all seven gates passed.
